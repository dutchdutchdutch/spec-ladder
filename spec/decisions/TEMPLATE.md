**Decision.** One or two sentences. What we are doing, stated plainly.

**Why.** The reasoning, including what we knew and didn't know at the time.

**What would change my mind.** The observable condition that should make us
revisit. Be specific enough that an agent could check it.

**Rejected.** The alternatives considered and why each lost. Keep these — they
are what stops the same debate recurring.

**Consequences.** What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what has to
change if it's reversed.
